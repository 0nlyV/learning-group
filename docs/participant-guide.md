# Participant guide

## Find a journey

Open the **Learning Group · Portal** post in a participating subreddit. It groups currently active journeys separately from concluded journeys and shows each journey's start date, session count, and community participant count.

Select **Open journey** to visit its Reddit post. Every journey has its own discussion and progress record.

## Learn at your own pace

1. Select **Begin the journey** or **Continue learning**.
2. Review any shared resources shown above **Your path**. On desktop, right-click a URL and choose **Open link in new tab**. On mobile, press and hold it. Reddit client behaviour may determine what a normal tap does.
3. Work through the sessions in any timeframe. Each session contains guidance, an estimated duration, and a reflection prompt.
4. Use **Mark complete** when ready. Select **Completed** to reverse that choice.
5. After all sessions are complete, review the closing reflection and select **Join the discussion** to return to the post comments.

**Join the discussion** remains hidden until all sessions are complete. **Close learning view** is always available and returns to the post without changing progress.

## What other people can see

Other participants and moderators can see aggregate participation and per-session completion totals. They cannot use Learning Group to see a list of which accounts completed a particular session. Your personal completion state is restored only when you use the same Reddit account.

## Accessibility and appearance

The interface follows the device or Reddit light/dark appearance setting, adapts to narrow screens, and uses labelled controls, generous spacing, high-contrast colours, and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next. Participants who enable reduced motion in their device settings do not receive unnecessary motion effects.
